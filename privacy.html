Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Aim990 collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, no ads, and no tracking. Your study data stays on your device. Aim990 不收集任何個人資料:沒有帳號、沒有登入、沒有廣告、沒有追蹤。你的學習資料只留在你的裝置上。
TL;DR · 零資料收集

1. Data we collect

None. Aim990 does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or third-party trackers.

無。Aim990 不收集、不傳輸、不販售任何個人資訊,也不使用分析 SDK、廣告識別碼或第三方追蹤工具。

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress, answers, score goal, test date, streaks, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see this data. Deleting the app removes it.

  • Study progress, answers and accuracy
  • Score goal, test date and daily plan
  • Streaks, achievements and preferences

你的進度、作答、目標分數、考試日期、連續天數、成就與設定都只儲存在本機,我們無法看到。刪除 App 即會移除這些資料。

3. Purchases

Aim990 offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium features. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. Purchase validation uses Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Aim990 提供一次性 App 內購買以解鎖進階功能。付款全程由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理,我們不會取得你的付款資訊;購買驗證使用裝置上的 Apple StoreKit。

4. Speech & audio

Listening practice uses on-device text-to-speech provided by iOS. Audio is generated locally and is not recorded or uploaded.

聽力練習使用 iOS 裝置內建的文字轉語音,音訊在本機產生,不會被錄製或上傳。

5. Children's privacy

Aim990 does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, the app is safe to use without sharing personal information.

Aim990 不會收集任何人(包含兒童)的資料。由於完全不收集資料,使用本 App 無須提供個人資訊。

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Because the app collects no data, material changes are highly unlikely.

政策若有變動,將更新本頁上方日期。因 App 不收集資料,重大變動的可能性極低。

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hourstag.app@gmail.com.

有任何隱私相關問題,歡迎來信 hourstag.app@gmail.com。
