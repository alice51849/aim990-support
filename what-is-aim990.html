What is Aim990?

Aim990 is a diagnostic coach for TOEIC Listening & Reading style preparation, not a question dump. Every session you open is prescribed from your own answers. It starts with an 8-minute diagnostic of about 20 grammar, listening and reading tasks, names your three weakest skills, turns them into a written plan, has you practice one skill a day in three phases (diagnose, fix, verify) and re-measures the same skills on day 30.

Who is it for?

It suits learners preparing for the TOEIC Listening & Reading format who would rather find out exactly where they lose points than work through endless random sets. It also includes an emergency mode for the last 1, 3, 7 or 14 days before a test, timed grammar drills with hints after 15 seconds, timing practice for long passages, dictation and shadowing, and spaced-repetition vocabulary with meanings in your language.

How does it cost?

There is a single one-time purchase that unlocks everything, with no subscription and no renewal. Check the current price on the App Store page, since it can vary by country. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking; your data stays on your device and daily practice works offline.
