English weak spots: find your three, then fix them

If your English practice feels busy but your results stay flat, the problem is usually not effort. It is that most of the practice lands on skills you already have. This page explains how Aim990: Fix English Weak Spots finds your three weakest skills for TOEIC Listening & Reading-style study, how it repairs them, and what the one-time purchase unlocks.

1. Find your weak spots in about 8 minutes

Aim990 starts with an 8-minute diagnosis: about 20 grammar, listening and reading tasks. From your answers it estimates a practice band and names your three weakest skills. A weakness radar shows those three repair targets, and an evidence ledger keeps recording accuracy and pace for every skill as you go.

2. Fix them one skill a day

Your three weak spots become a written plan: what to repair first, why it is costing you points and how long it should take. Each day drills one prescribed skill in three phases: diagnose, repair, verify. There are no endless random sets.

  • Grammar speed drills with 15-second clues
  • A reading time trainer for long passages
  • Listening dictation and shadowing with on-device speech
  • Spaced-repetition vocabulary with meanings in your language

3. Check what actually moved on day 30

On day 30 the same skills are measured again, so you can put day 1 evidence next to day 30 evidence and see what changed. There is also a practice mock with an estimated practice band, and a Panic Mode for the final 1, 3, 7 or 14 days before a test.
