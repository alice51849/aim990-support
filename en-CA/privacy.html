Privacy Policy

Aim990

How Aim990 handles information, where eligible data stays, and which choices remain under your control.

Last updated: 2026-08-29

Data we collect

None. Aim990 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. There is no developer-run account or sign-in system.

Data on your device

Content and settings created in Aim990 stay in the app's local storage on your device. Removing the app removes its local app data; files you have deliberately exported remain wherever you saved them.

Recognition

Text, speech, or image recognition covered by this policy runs on your device. Recognised content is not sent to a developer server.

Purchases

The optional one-time in-app purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive payment details, and there is no recurring subscription.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Aim990 contains no third-party advertising, analytics, attribution, or tracking SDK. The developer does not build a cross-app or cross-site profile.

Children's privacy

Because Aim990 does not collect personal data, it does not collect a child's personal data. It does not ask the developer for a child's name, birth date, contact details, photos, or health information.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page and its last-updated date will be revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hourstag.app@gmail.com.
